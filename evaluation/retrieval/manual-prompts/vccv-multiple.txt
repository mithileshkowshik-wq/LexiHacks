# Graduated Error-Correction and Retrieval Support

Recurring prompts distinguish an unsupported wrong answer from a stable skill gap:

- Ask for the phonogram sound or position.
- Prompt for a keyword; show the keyword/image if retrieval fails.
- Give the sound only after retrieval prompts fail.
- Trace and sound three times after the sound is retrieved or supplied.
- Blend incrementally before reading the whole word.
- Use fingerspelling for phonetic spelling errors.
- Identify morphemes and syllable boundaries for more advanced words.
- Prompt capitalization and punctuation separately from phonetic spelling.
- Supply learned/sight-word spellings where the task is testing another target.

The recommendation engine should therefore distinguish the observed error from the amount and type of prompting required. ^[inferred]

# Cumulative Scope and Sequence

The materials repeatedly review previous concepts and carry known sound patterns into later drills. The sampled Year 1 Term 1 sequence moves from foundational sound-symbol work toward short vowels, consonant confusions, syllable types, long vowels, morphology, vocabulary, comprehension, and increasingly extended writing.

A resource can match an error topically and still be inappropriate when its prerequisite sound, syllable, morphology, language, or response demand is not secure. Resource cards therefore expose both target skills and prerequisites. Exact programme progression beyond the supplied term remains incomplete. ^[ambiguous]
